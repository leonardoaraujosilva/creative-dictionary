## 🎮 GAME OVERVIEW

**Creative Dictionary** is a vibrant, witty, and engaging party board game powered by a web application. Players take turns inventing clever, convincing, or hilarious definitions for extremely rare, erudite, and obscure words. The goal isn't necessarily to know the "correct" dictionary definition, but to fool your friends, guess the right one, and race your player token across the visual board track to claim victory!

---

## 📱 WHAT IS A "PASS-AND-PLAY" (HOTSEAT) GAME?

**Creative Dictionary** uses a local multiplayer system known as **Pass-and-Play** (or *Hotseat*). 

This means **you only need a single device** (smartphone, tablet, or laptop) to play with your entire group! 
* Players take turns using the same screen.
* During the voting phase, the app uses a **Pass Screen** to ensure privacy.
* You pass the device to the next player, maintaining total secrecy when reading definitions and casting votes.

## 🔄 GAMEPLAY FLOW & ROUND STRUCTURE

Each round follows an interactive pass-and-play sequence:

### 1. Language & Player Setup
* Select your preferred language flag on the home screen.
* Enter all participating players (**minimum of 3 required**). Press **Enter** or click **Add**. If you make a typo, you can instantly remove a player using the `✕` button next to their name.
* The game dynamically calculates and displays the victory goal right on screen as you add players (**4 points per player**, starting at 12 points).

### 2. The Rare Word & Writing Phase (Non-Sequential)
* A rare, obscure erudite word is presented for the round.
* Instead of following a strict fixed order, a live dashboard shows who still needs to write.
* **Non-Sequential Mechanics:** The player who thinks of a definition first grabs the phone, clicks their name, types their clever definition in secret, and returns instantly to the menu.

### 3. Anonymous Voting Phase
* All definitions are shuffled anonymously.
* Players pass the phone around to vote on the definition they believe is best or most creative. Players **cannot** vote for their own definition.

### 4. Scoring & Visual Board Track
* Points are awarded based on votes received (+1 point per vote).
* Your player token advances forward along the persistent visual board track fixed at the bottom of the screen.
* **Full Scroll Support:** The board track features both mobile touch-scrolling and desktop drag-to-scroll / mouse wheel support, with an automatic scroll assistant that keeps the current leader visible near the right edge.

---

## 🏆 VICTORY CONDITION & DYNAMIC SCORING

* **Dynamic Point Goal:** The winning score scales automatically based on the number of active players: **4 points per player**, starting at a minimum of **12 points** (for 3 players) up to a hard maximum cap of **30 points**.
  * *Examples:* 
    * 3 players = 12 points
    * 4 players = 16 points
    * 5 players = 20 points
    * 8+ players = capped at 30 points.
* **Winning the Game:** The first player to reach or exceed the target point goal on the board track instantly wins the game and is crowned the **Master Lexicographer**!
